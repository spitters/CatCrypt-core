\chapter{Worked example: CTR mode}

Following Rosulek, \emph{The Joy of Cryptography}, §8 (block-cipher modes).
Counter mode encrypts a block $m$ as $(\mathit{nonce}, E(k, \mathit{nonce}) \oplus
m)$ with fresh randomness. Single-block CTR is exactly the CPA-from-PRF
construction with the PRF input fixed to the counter, so a bijection block cipher
makes it perfectly IND-CPA.

\begin{definition}[Single-block CTR]
  \label{def:ctr}
  \uses{def:cpafromprf}
  \lean{CatCrypt.Examples.CTRMode.CTRBlockScheme, CatCrypt.Examples.CTRMode.CTRBlockScheme.toBijCPAScheme}
  \leanok
  A CTR block scheme is a keystream bijection family $\mathrm{Block} \to
  (\mathrm{Key} \simeq \mathrm{Block})$ modelling $k \mapsto E(k, \mathit{ctr})$;
  it maps directly onto the PRF-based encryption scheme.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Perfect IND-CPA]
  \label{thm:ctr-perfect}
  \uses{def:ctr, thm:cpafromprf-perfect}
  \lean{CatCrypt.Examples.CTRMode.ctr_perfect_indcpa, CatCrypt.Examples.CTRMode.boolCTR_perfect_indcpa}
  \leanok
  Single-block CTR from a bijection block cipher has perfect IND-CPA security:
  advantage exactly $0$, inherited from the CPA-from-PRF result. (Multi-block CTR
  needs a product-of-bijections argument and is out of scope.)
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Computational bound from a PRF]
  \label{thm:ctr-bound}
  \uses{def:ctr, thm:cpafromprf-bound}
  \lean{CatCrypt.Examples.CTRMode.ctr_indcpa_bound}
  \leanok
  For a general block cipher with PRF-advantage $\varepsilon$, single-block CTR has
  $\mathsf{Adv}^{\mathrm{IND\text{-}CPA}} \le \varepsilon$, routing through the same
  PRF-swap and perfect hops as CPA-from-PRF.
\end{theorem}
